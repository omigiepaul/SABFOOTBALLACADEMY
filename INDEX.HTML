Welcome to Dazzle Football Academy

About Us

Founded in 2009, Dazzle Football Academy is committed to nurturing and promoting Nigeria’s finest young football talents. Our mission is to develop players with exceptional skills, discipline, and character, preparing them for professional careers in top clubs locally and globally. Through world-class training, mentorship, and exposure to scouts, we transform raw talent into global stars.

Our Motto

Building Future Pro

Our Vision

To be Africa’s premier football academy, shaping global stars who excel on the pitch and inspire in their communities through discipline, innovation, and passion.

Our Statement

At Dazzle Football Academy, we transform dreams into reality by nurturing young talent with world-class training, fostering character, and creating opportunities for success in football and beyond.

Facilities

Home Ground: Idiagbon Primary School, Ifako-Ijaiye

Located in Ifako-Ijaiye, Lagos, our home ground at Idiagbon Primary School is where we host competitive matches and engage with the local community.

View Idiagbon Primary School on Google Maps

Training Center: First African Church Mission Primary School, Iju Ishaga

Located in Iju Ishaga, Lagos, our training center at First African Church Mission Primary School provides top-tier facilities for skill development, fitness, and tactical training, fostering the growth of our players. Training sessions are held from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, though the schedule may vary due to matches on any day.
